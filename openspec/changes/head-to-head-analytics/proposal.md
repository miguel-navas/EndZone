# Head-to-head analytics
## Why
Deliver FR-010–013 and FR-015 under ADR-001, with historical and current facts in PostgreSQL.
## Scope
Compare two distinct franchise IDs, direct encounters and independent overall performance, season ranges, regular/postseason/Super Bowl filters, six tabs, paginated game history, coverage and provenance. No preseason by default. No AI-generated facts.
## Dependencies
Normalized data model, validated ingestion, franchise mapping and source licensing. See docs/architecture/data-model.md.
## Out of scope
Player/coach comparison, live predictions, betting, production provisioning.
